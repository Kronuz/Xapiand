allocates a chunk per record. `Shard::reader_records_probe()` reports `allocated()` after the shard is gone, and
`test_a_teardown_allocates_no_reader_records` (capacity 4096) fails with the old walk (`CHECK(probe() == before)`) and
passes with the fix. Xapiand keeps its gate at `readers_per_index` until X3-2.
